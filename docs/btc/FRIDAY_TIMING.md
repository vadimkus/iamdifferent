## Interpretation

### Why 16:00 Dubai wins
- 7 AM Eastern is US pre-market, when BTC liquidity is transitioning from Asian session
- Low-volatility window before US equity markets open at 9:30 AM ET
- Gives maximum time for the TP +0.5% to hit during the US session
- 72.1% of the time, BTC moves +0.5% from this entry within 24 hours

### Scaling Strategy
For a large position ($270K), consider splitting:
- **50% at 16:00 Dubai** — best TP hit rate (72.1%)
- **50% at 20:00 Dubai** — lowest SL hit rate (25.0%)

This gives you the best of both: high probability on the first half, low risk on the second.

### Late Entry (20:00-00:00 Dubai)
If you miss the 16:00 window:
- 20:00 Dubai is the safest entry (lowest stop-loss hit at 25%)
- After 22:00 Dubai, win rates drop below 50% — avoid
- 03:00 Dubai (23:00 UTC) is a sleeper pick: lowest SL at 19.4% but lower TP hit
